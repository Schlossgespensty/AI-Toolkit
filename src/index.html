Castle shortcuts

Select a field and press a key, optionally with Ctrl, Alt or Shift. One shortcut per action; each combination can only be assigned once. Backspace clears a field. Unassigned actions remain available in the UI.

Camera

Ctrl+scroll pans horizontally. In wheel-zoom mode, Alt+scroll or Shift+scroll pans vertically. Middle-drag pans. Camera keys also accept Ctrl/Alt/Shift combinations; Backspace clears. Hold Shift to pan faster.
